Synagogue Name-Tag Printing System

Welcome every member and guest, by name.

A self-service welcome kiosk for your lobby. Visitors and members scan a QR code, type their name, and a printed name badge is ready in seconds — no volunteer at a table, no app to download, no handwriting on sticky labels.

What it does

Simple and self-service

  • Works on any phone through a QR code — nothing to install
  • Separate member and visitor flows, each asking only what it needs
  • Family sign-in prints a badge for everyone in the household at once

Warm and inclusive

  • Optional pronouns field you can switch on for Pride or any event
  • Your congregation's logo and wording on every badge
  • Optional guest photo capture for a friendly welcome record

Easy for the office

  • Attendance log with date filters and one-click Excel export
  • Reprint, test-print, and manage printers from a simple admin console
  • Run several kiosks — lobby, religious school, social hall

Connects to your systems

Integrates with your member-tracking software.

Already rolling out new membership or CRM software? Sign-ins can flow straight into it through a simple integration, so your welcome desk and your member records stay in step automatically.
